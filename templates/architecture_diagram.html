ElevateBox voice agent: call in, decisions out, WhatsApp while it's still ringing

One outbound call, one live decision engine, two different WhatsApp sends triggered at two different moments — send_whatsapp_now / book_callback mid-call, end_call_summary once the line goes dead. The timing is the point: everything drawn in blue below fires while the caller is still on the phone.

Fires mid-call

  • Hot or Cold read: classify_lead() + enough of update_discovery() (4/5 fields) fires send_whatsapp_now — as a background task, never blocking the reply.
  • A named callback time — "tomorrow morning," "Thursday afternoon" — runs through scheduling.parse_callback_time() and fires the confirmation via book_callback(), no discovery gate required.
  • Both dedupe on one instance flag: exactly one mid-call WhatsApp per call, regardless of how many times classification updates.

Fires after hang_up()

  • end_call_summary() assembles the full discovery context, the classification tag, and any booked callback time into one paragraph.
  • _finish_call() waits for any in-flight mid-call send, sends the summary, then removes the SIP participant — summary always goes out, win or lose the pitch.
  • Carries the four required extras: your number, resume link, and this architecture image.
